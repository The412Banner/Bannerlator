lunzip bundled as liblunzip.so
==============================

Purpose
-------
Decompresses lzip files (.lz, .tlz, .tar.lz) for BFE's Unpack screen (ExtraFormats engine). BFE
feeds the file on stdin and writes the output straight to the destination; a resulting .tar is
then unpacked by the bundled 7-Zip.
