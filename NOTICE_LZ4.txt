lz4 command-line tool bundled as liblz4.so
==========================================

Purpose
-------
Decompresses LZ4 files (.lz4, .tar.lz4 — LZ4 frame format, concatenated frames, and the legacy
format) for BFE's Unpack screen (ExtraFormats engine), fed on stdin; a resulting .tar is then
unpacked by the bundled 7-Zip.
