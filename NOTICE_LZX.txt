lzx (amiga-lzx) bundled as liblzx.so
====================================

Purpose
-------
Extracts Amiga LZX archives (.lzx — Jonathan Forbes' Amiga archiver, not Microsoft's CAB LZX)
for BFE's Unpack screen. It refuses absolute paths, ".." components and symlinks in archive
names, so nothing lands outside the chosen folder.

Why not unlzx.c: the classic unlzx.c carries no licence statement anywhere (distributions list it
as "unknown"), so BFE doesn't ship it. amiga-lzx is an independent reimplementation under a free
licence.
