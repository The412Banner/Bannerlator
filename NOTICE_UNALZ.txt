unalz bundled as libunalz.so
============================

Purpose
-------
Extracts ALZip .alz archives (Korean ALZip's older format) for BFE's Unpack screen, converting the
CP949 file names to UTF-8.

File in this APK (app/src/main/jniLibs/arm64-v8a/)
  libunalz.so   unalz 0.65 statically linked with GNU libiconv 1.17 and the NDK's zlib, fully STATIC
                aarch64 Android (bionic) executable, stripped. 1,684,912 bytes,
                sha256 b9324d089d9835a8c8d0bf419365e5e89206d0599b8e15b2f82c0d605c81b073
