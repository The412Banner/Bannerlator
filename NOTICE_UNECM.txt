unecm (ecm-tools) bundled as libunecm.so
========================================

Purpose
-------
Rebuilds CD images from ECM files (game.bin.ecm -> game.bin): ECM strips the sync, header and
error-correction (EDC/ECC) bytes that can be recomputed from the sector data, and unecm puts them
back. BFE also copies a matching .cue next to the result.

                Android (bionic) executable, stripped. 423,232 bytes, sha256 ee927578945a53602d0f2c57b27003e36d22f8aaa4ee68ad60ef9a0bc5ab7202

Modification (native/patches/ecm-tools/0001-decode-only.patch, by The412Banner): the program picks
encode or decode from its own name (argv[0] == "ecm2bin"); shipped as libunecm.so that can never
match, so main() always decodes. Nothing else is changed.

-----

patch, then <ndk clang> -O2 -static -o unecm ecm.c.
