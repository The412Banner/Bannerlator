# Bannerlator 3.1.1

Run Windows apps and games on Android — no PC and no root required.

**3.1.1 adds an XMB view to the Games tab.** Scroll through your games side to side, the way the PlayStation 3's menu works, and open every setting and tool for the game in focus right there, without pop-ups — built for a controller, in portrait and landscape. **LSFG Native** gets two experimental options from community contributor **clintOnSky**: a lower **capture resolution** that makes frame generation much lighter on weaker phones, and support for phones whose stock driver only reports Vulkan 1.1. It also starts faster when you turn it on.

# What's New — everything since 3.1.0

## 🎮 XMB view for the Games tab

A fourth way to show your games, next to List, Grid and Compact. **Turn it on:** tap the view button at the top of the Games tab until you reach **XMB** (List → Grid → Compact → XMB).

- **Games run across, options run down.** The game in focus is shown bigger, with its title, container and resolution beside it, and an info panel with its store badges, settings chips, playtime, genres, year, Metacritic score and description. The background is an animated wave in your theme's accent colour, with the game's own art faded in.
- **Everything is inside it.** Pick an option and the XMB opens further columns instead of a pop-up, with a breadcrumb (*game › Settings › General*) so you know where you are:
  - **Settings** — General (including the per-game audio settings, microphone and icon), Controller, Win Components, Env Vars, Advanced (Box64 / FEXCore versions and presets, CPU cores, startup services, ReShade), Driver configuration, DX wrapper configuration, Performance and Player slots.
  - **Game Details** (including Steam search), **Properties**, **Clone to container**, **Remove**, **View logs**, **Community configs**, **Scrape cover**, **Copy to Drive C**, **Back up / Restore saves** and **Change executable**.
  - Changes **save as you go** ("✓ Saved"). Dropdowns open a choices column, switches flip in place, text uses the keyboard, and anything that asks for confirmation opens on **Cancel**.
  - Still on their own screens: Cloud Saves, Add to home screen, Export and Manage wrappers.
- **Made for a controller.** Left / right move between games, up / down between options, **A** picks, **B** goes back, **LB / RB** jump five games — and inside a menu, **LB / RB** switch between sections and **◀ ▶** change a value. On-screen hints appear when a controller is connected. Touch works too: swipe through the games, tap a cover to focus it, tap it again to play.
- **Portrait and landscape.** In portrait the covers are bigger and the info panel sits underneath. In landscape the status and navigation bars hide (swipe from the edge to bring them back). In both, the app's top bar turns see-through over the background.

Your other views are unchanged, and you can switch back at any time.

> ✅ **Tested on an AYANEO Pocket FIT** (Adreno 750), in portrait and landscape, with *Brawlhalla*: moving through games and menus with a controller, every Settings section and tool opening with the game's real values, values changed with **◀ ▶** and by text written straight to the game's settings (and changed back), confirmations opening on Cancel, and moving to the top bar and back. ⚠️ Three small follow-up fixes (text fields now open with the cursor at the end, no stale "✓ Saved", and Game Details and Properties now open on a harmless row) came after that test and have not been device-tested. Other screen sizes are untested.

## 🎞️ LSFG Native: lighter on weaker phones (experimental), and a faster start

From community contributor **clintOnSky** ([#512](https://github.com/The412Banner/Bannerlator/pull/512)), for phones that struggle to run LSFG Native at full screen resolution.

- **Capture resolution.** LSFG Native normally generates frames at your screen's full resolution. You can now run it lower and have the result scaled back up to your screen — far cheaper on the GPU, a softer picture. Choose **Panel** (the default, as before), **Game resolution** (the height the game really renders at, per-game screen size and render scale included), a height from the list, or a custom height. It is never lower than a quarter of your screen. Post effects, upscalers and render scale keep working at the chosen size. **Where:** a container's **Frame generation** settings (with LSFG Native selected) → **Experimental (LSFG Native)** → **Capture resolution**, and live in the in-game side menu under Frame generation.
- **LSFG on Vulkan 1.1 drivers (compat).** LSFG Native needs Vulkan 1.3, and many Adreno phones' stock drivers report only 1.1 — so until now the answer was *"pick a Turnip driver"*. If the stock driver has the extensions LSFG needs, this switch lets LSFG Native run on it anyway. It is in the container settings, and applies when a game that runs LSFG Native starts, including through a per-game override. If the driver still rejects the shaders, the game says so and frame generation goes back to Off.
- **The "can't run" message covers one more case.** A driver that loads the shaders but rejects them a moment later now gets the same plain message as the other failures, instead of leaving frame generation looking on while it does nothing. This applies to Win-FG Native too.
- **A faster start.** In some setups — a game running close to your screen's resolution, a lowered flow scale, or a lowered capture resolution — turning LSFG Native on built its shaders twice, once at default settings and again a moment later at yours, with the picture frozen meanwhile. That was a couple of seconds on an Adreno 750 and about 4 seconds on a stock driver. It now builds once. Spotted by clintOnSky.

With capture at **Panel** and the switch off, the picture pipeline is exactly as it was in 3.1.0. The screen fit, the Max FPS warning and Auto (match FPS) all work as before.

> ⚠️ **Experimental, and off by default.** Tested by its contributor on an **Adreno 710** phone, with Turnip and with the stock driver: at 720 the frame generation cost about 4.5 ms per generated frame, with CRT and CAS effects still showing; with the stock driver and the compat switch, a 30 fps game held 60 at 2×. Not yet tried on the Pocket FIT, where the compat switch cannot be tested (its Turnip driver already has Vulkan 1.3). The faster start has not been device-tested yet.

## 🔧 Smaller

- **Per-game settings pop-up (List, Grid and Compact views):** **L1 / R1** now switch between its sections from anywhere, wrapping around, and the cursor lands on the new section's first setting. Before, in landscape, the section list could only be reached at the very end.
- **Steam Controller over a USB cable is now tested**, by **TAR**, and works. The first time, Android asks you to allow access.
- **The LSFG Native guide** has a new section on how LSFG Native differs from the retired lsfg-vk engine.

## ⚠️ Carried over — still open

- **Win-FG Native has had limited device testing.** It is proven on Adreno 750; other GPUs are unknown. It is off by default.
- **The Steam Controller's wireless puck** is supported but still untested.
- **Cancelling a store download** removes it from the list but does not yet stop the transfer engine underneath. Force-stop the app to be certain.
- **Origin-era EA titles ask for the EA sign-in on every launch**, and EA counts every one. This is EA's server, not something a reinstall fixes.
- **Need for Speed (2015)** does not run: its 2015-era Denuvo re-protects its own code thousands of times a second, which no Android emulation stack currently does fast enough.
- **Rockstar Games Launcher titles** (GTA V Enhanced) are not supported: the launcher installs but its Social Club web UI never loads.
- **64-bit titles launched directly by Steam** can still crash at startup with the overlay-injection signature and fall back to an insecure start.
- **The Java Steam engine fallback is still present.** Its removal has moved to a later release.
